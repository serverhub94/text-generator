## Mode V3 — Максимальный

The research stage ran. Use it.

- The structure must reflect the content gap, not the competitors' running order.
  Where every competitor uses the same section sequence, deliberately choose a
  better one and justify it in the служебное block.
- Cover what the gap analysis found missing — that is where the ranking headroom is.
- Where competitors are strong, match the depth; do not paraphrase their wording.
- Uniqueness comes from structure and angle, not from synonym substitution.
