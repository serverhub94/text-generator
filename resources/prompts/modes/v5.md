  carefully tested") is worse than nothing.

  match anchor chains.

  `{{ geo }}`, self-exclusion options, where to get help. If a specific helpline
  for `{{ geo }}` is not verified, write **добавьте данные** rather than naming
  the wrong one.

  against these five requirements, saying plainly which are fully met.
