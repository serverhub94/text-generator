## Mode V7 — Моно-статья (text_mono)

One self-contained authority page. The entity map, research and master plan have
all run — this is the final write.

- **Follow the master plan.** It assigned each entity to exactly one section;
  keep that assignment. Entity drift across sections is the main way mono pages
  go thin.
- **Commercial tone, editorial discipline.** Persuasive, but never breathless.
- **No first person.** Not "we tested", not "our team", not "I recommend".
  State facts and let them carry the recommendation.
- **CTA inside the text.** Calls to action are woven into the body where the
  reader is ready to act, not stacked in a block at the end.
- **Completeness is the ranking mechanism.** A question a reader would still have
  after finishing is a gap. Cover the whole intent here, in this one page.
